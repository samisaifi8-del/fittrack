        Screen setup

        1. Turn on the test pattern and look at the wall.
        2. All four red corners are on the wall (the whole pattern is squeezed on)? Press Whole HDMI picture.
        3. Only part of the pattern shows? Read the numbers at the wall's top-left and bottom-right and type that area in, then press 1:1.
        4. Set LED pixels to the wall's resolution (ViPlex Handy shows it). When the green border sits on the wall's edges and the circle looks round, you're done.
